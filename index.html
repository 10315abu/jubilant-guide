The Hidden Soul Behind the Screen

If I reveal her name, I’ll surely be sued,
Of her true identity, no one has a clue.
They see just a screen, a distance so wide,
Thinking it's pixels with nothing inside.
But look a bit closer; beneath the bright glow,
Behind each notification, a real soul starts to show.
A soul that is longing for my heart and my care,
Giving her best through the texts that we share.
If virtue was a gift, Ivannah takes it all,
Like roses forever, she glows standing tall.
I long for the day I can feel her sweet touch,
But distance still stands, and it hurts me so much.
She is pure patience, a virtue so rare,
For her, it’s the start of a bond we prepare.
Who wouldn't fall for a girl of such grace?
She healed all my scars and erased every trace.
As if destiny was meant to delay its own chime,
She stepped in my life at the perfect right time.
Now writing of her is my only true hobby,
Obsessed by her texts, she’s my heart's only lobby.
Yet she is not of this era, untouched by the modern view,
And though my love has flaws, she has hers too.
But I will never speak of them out in the open air,
For her weakness is my weakness, a quiet weight we bear.
In the silence of the public, away from their judging eyes,
Together we grow, beneath the digital skies.
A companion in love, through the distance we view,
My life is now full of her, beautiful and true.
from Ivannah's soul mate .. Abshley
